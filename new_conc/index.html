Professional Master’s: Where Your Career Gets Its Advanced Degree

Forget choosing between theory and practice. The Professional Master’s is designed for the driven professional who demands a qualification that works as hard as they do.

This is not a traditional academic program. It’s a career accelerator built from the ground up for immediate impact. We replace abstract theories with applied competencies, and generic subjects with a functional, domain-based curriculum curated by industry experts.

Areas of Professional Master’s

Explore flexible, industry-driven pathways designed for future-ready learners.

Available

Professional Master’s in Applied Human Resource Management (PMAHRM)

It delivers globally recognised vocational excellence grounded in rigorous standards, ensuring that learners gain not only cutting-edge HR expertise but also a qualification with strong endorsement for credibility and career advancement.

Available

Professional Master’s in Applied Finance and Accounting (PMAFA)

Develop finance professionals' expertise in core areas like accounting, financial planning, cost management, compliance, and risk assessment. Based on eight key functional domains, it combines practical skills with strategic thinking.

Upcoming

Professional Master’s in Applied Leadership & OD

Builds advanced leadership capability with a strong focus on organizational development, enabling professionals to lead change, enhance performance, and strengthen workplace culture.

Available

Professional Master’s in Applied Project Management (PMAPM)

Equips professionals with practical leadership skills in complex projects, combining global standards with real-world application across key areas like scope, budget, risk, and stakeholder engagement to improve their project management and leadership capabilities.

Upcoming

Professional Master’s in Applied Supply Chain Management

Provides in-depth knowledge of modern supply chain operations, equipping learners with practical skills to optimize processes, manage logistics, and improve overall efficiency.

Upcoming

Professional Master’s in Applied HSE Management

Focuses on advanced health, safety, and environmental practices, preparing professionals to manage risk, ensure regulatory compliance, and create safer, sustainable workplaces.
